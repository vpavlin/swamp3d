# 0007. Free models and tips first; paid models in phase 2

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-05

## Context

Payments add the most complexity (LEZ testnet only, fair exchange unsolved, no fiat off-ramp) and nothing to the theft story.

## Decision

Phase 1: free models; optional public tips via LEZ. Phase 2: paid models via a `purchase` program, file key delivered by the seller or their hub over Messaging. Phase 3: deliver-or-refund escrow, arbiters.

## Rejected

Paid models from day one.

## Consequences

The first users are people who publish for free anyway, i.e. most of the existing community.
